A press in the sensor readings

With the pad untouched, the five chips measure the magnets’ field in three directions each. This gives a 15-number reading at rest. A press changes the reading because the magnets move inside the pad. The same force at two surface points moves the magnets differently, so the pattern across the chips helps locate the press.

The finite-element model predicts magnet motion for a force at each point on the surface. The field model converts that motion into a predicted reading. The estimator compares a new reading with those predictions and returns a point and a force. The simulated errors below test that chain using readings generated by the models. The later UR5 presses compare its estimates with measured positions and forces.
