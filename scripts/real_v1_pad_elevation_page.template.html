Pad contact elevation and tool retention across eight deployed hands

Retracted on 2026-09-06: the claimed rise from 18 to 30 full chains of 48 with a pad-target angle counted a screwdriver standing on its handle as upright, and on the signed cosine {{HANDLE_DOWN}} of the {{TOTAL_STANDS}} table stands were handle down. Replaced by the control diagnosis of the same date
Retraction

probe_real_v1_chain._av() folds the tool’s axis to point up when tip_len is 0, and real_v1_chain_hands._cell passes tip_len=0.0 for every table-stand cell, so tilt_deg is arccos|cos| and a screwdriver standing on its handle reads as a 0.00° stand. Every stand count on the original page came from that mode. Re-scored on the signed cosine, {{HANDLE_DOWN}} of {{TOTAL_STANDS}} of those stands were handle down, 180° from the task, which voids the elevation sweep, the grip-loop sweep and the straddle×depth grid. The air-mode results were signed and stand: 0 stands in 96.

The drops come from the pivot height axis_k, which decides which end of the tool goes down, and from the grasp fitter, which leaves the fingers no extension to turn with (control-diagnosis page).

Terms and metrics

z_rel
Height of a pad contact above the shaft’s own axis at closure, in shaft radii; negative is below the equator. The table gives the mean of the three pads.
Span
Angle around the shaft covered by the three contacts, in degrees.
Fz,pad
Vertical component of the summed pad contact force at closure, in N; positive holds the tool up.
Δz at close
Vertical displacement of the tool while the grasp closes, in mm.
Carry (chain), bench retention
Chain runs, of 6, that keep the tool through the carry; bench trials on the CB1 that kept the tool through a completed reorientation.
Signed cosine
Cosine of the tool’s own +z axis with world +z: +1 tip down, −1 handle down.
