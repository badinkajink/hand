Signed cosine of the tool’s axis with vertical (+1 upright); held: the tool above 60 mm with at least 0.5 N on a pad at the last step, counted over 64 rollouts.
Perturbed reset, perturbed plant
Evaluation with the tool’s spawn pose jittered; evaluation on plants with changed gain, friction or mass that the policy did not train on.
Grip term
A reward penalty of −2 on pad force above 6 N per pad, the servos’ measured holding force.
Index–middle clearance
Smallest surface distance between the index and middle link chains, in mm.

The two arms, the observation and the residual clip

Every policy here is a sighted actor: its 66-dimensional observation includes the tool pose (object_pos, object_pose_actual, target_axis_misalign), which the AprilTag tracker would supply at 50 Hz on the bench. With those terms zeroed in CPU MuJoCo, both sighted 60 M D6 checkpoints drop the tool in 4 of 4 rollouts. A blind actor warm-started from the sighted checkpoint (--actor-blind-terms) did not recover the hold in 22 iterations and was stopped.

The unclipped D6 finetune of 2026-09-17 commands targets up to 384° beyond the joint range, which on a real servo is a stall into the overload cutoff, so every job clips the raw actions to ±2 (±1 rad of residual, --clip-actions 2.0, recorded in the run’s config so evaluation applies it). The second arm penalises index–middle link clearance below 30 mm (weight −10, quadratic in the shortfall), because the built fingers carry servo housings and cabling the capsules omit; the D6 grip finetune runs them 18 mm apart at its closest.

The policy inside the UR5e chain

real_v1_chain_policy.py --stages policy: the UR5e holds the hand through its 100 mm wrist stack; grasp closure, a 100 mm arm lift and a wait follow the training schedule, and the policy then drives the fingers at 50 Hz for 5 s · plant cal, plate at the built 25 mm and at 0 · one nominal rollout each

{{TABLE_CHAIN}}

The menagerie UR5e servo is overdamped (slow pole 5 rad/s): 0.52 s after the lift the palm was still 4.9 mm below its command, so every chain rollout before 2026-09-20 handed the policy a settling arm. The chain now runs the arm at ten times the position gain (arm_kp_scale 10; 0.004 mm below command through the turn), and the finger bodies carry their own weight on the finger servos again (finger_gravcomp 0), as in the training scene and on the bench.

With these changes the observation the policy receives at its first active step in the chain matches the benchmark’s to within 1° on every finger joint, 1.3 mm on the tool position and 0.015 on the relative quaternion (D6, separation arm, obs_at_active in both JSONs); the only remaining scene difference is the screw-tip mesh on the chain’s tool, the “tip mesh” column of the zero-shot table. Future work

The 2026-09-20 continuation trains every checkpoint further on the chain’s plant with spawn jitter. These items remain open:

  1. A policy-driven controller on the station: the 66-dimensional observation from servo telemetry and the AprilTag pose at 50 Hz, the residual added to the finger set-points. It needs the tracker’s x/y heading calibrated first, because the policy reads the tool position in the palm frame.
  2. The training scene with the tip mesh (build_screw_scene.py on the flat scene before make_cal_morphology_run.py); if the tip mesh causes the chain drops, the tip-mesh column then matches the as-trained column.
  3. The GPU evaluator started from D1’s CPU-replayed initial state (or the CPU rollout from the GPU’s step-0 state). If D1 then holds on both, the six GPU–CPU disagreements are a reconstruction error; if it drops, the policies are fragile and the CPU held fraction across the perturbation columns ranks bench candidates.
  4. A caliper measurement of the closest index–middle approach on the built hand during a bench turn, to replace the 30 mm threshold, then a sweep of the clearance weight from −3 to −10 on D4 and D7, the two hands whose turn the penalty removes (cos 0.81; 4 and 0 of 64 aligned).
  5. A D8 grasp search on the calibrated plant, or training from the arm-lift reset: its fitted grasp drops the tool in the evaluator’s palm lift at 4–14 mm of squeeze.
  6. Three seeds per cell. The three D6 clip seeds end at cos 0.834, 0.965 and 0.962 (56, 64 and 64 of 64 held), so differences below 0.13 in cos or 8 of 64 in the hold between hands are not resolved.
