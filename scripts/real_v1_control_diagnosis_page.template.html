Effect of pivot height, residual clip and grasp fit on the deployed hands’ reorientation

Changing the one configuration that completes the chain (rv05_manual_stored, 169/169) toward the deployed plans one factor at a time identifies two causes of the lost reorientation. The plans’ pivot height turns the tool to the wrong pole (cos −0.988 at axis_k 0.05), and the geometric grasp fitter leaves the fingers 0.44–1.26 mm of extension headroom against the reference grasp’s 2.74 mm, so no squeeze completes the chain. With a per-hand pivot and a clip of 0.50, three of the eight deployed hands hold a tip-down, floor-free turn (D3 at cos +0.987); all three lose the tool at the set-down.

Ablation from the reproduced 2026-09-03 chain · 492 rollouts and a 96-rollout pivot sweep, filmed · signed cosine with a load test · simulation only

Terms and metrics

Chain
In simulation with a floating palm: lift the screwdriver from the table, turn it tip-down in the air, set it into the countersink and gait it 8 cycles. The count k/n is the number of rollouts, out of n, that complete it.
Reference chain
rv05_manual_stored with its stored CEM grasp, sphere pads, pivot 0.25, clip 0.50 and a −90° turn; 169/169 completed on 2026-09-03.
Seams
The chain’s checkpoints lifted, turned, reoriented, upright (set into the countersink) and gaited.
Signed cosine (cos)
Cosine of the tool’s own +z axis with world +z: +1 tip down, −1 handle down.
Pads / N
Pads in contact with the tool and their total force at a seam, mean over rollouts.
Held
Two or more pads carrying at least the tool’s 0.240 N weight at reoriented, the tool clear of the floor.
Pivot (axis_k)
Height of the point the finger anchor is rotated about during the turn, in half-straddles above the contact plane.
Clip
Largest excursion of a finger joint from the grasp anchor that the turn may command, in rad.
Squeeze
Commanded interference of the pads with the tool in the fitted grasp, in mm.
Extension, headroom
Mount-to-pad distance of each finger at the closed grip, in mm; headroom is full extension, 68.11 mm, minus the largest of the three.
Bench band
Range of clips, in rad, over which a plan holds the tool in the bench maneuver.
Tool height, lost at
Height of the tool at reoriented, in mm; the seam at which a run loses the tool.

Single-factor ablation from the reference

scripts/real_v1_chain_ablate.py · 32 rollouts

{{ABLATE_TBL}}

Pivot. At axis_k 0.05 the lift is the reference’s, since the pivot enters after it. The turn takes the tool to cos −0.988, within 9° of vertical but standing on its handle, and the tool drops. Every deployed plan sets 0.05 to 0.15.

Clip. At 0.85 the tool reaches cos +0.970 while the pad force falls from 5.55 N to 0.15 N, which is below the tool’s 0.240 N weight, so the tool arrives vertical in a hand that no longer holds it. The plan angle of −60° under-rotates and still chains 3 of 4.

Pads and grasp. Both pad arms fail at the lift, with no contacts, because each grasp was fitted for the other pad shape. Flat pads with a grasp fitted for them (last row) lift on three pads at 3.37 N and turn to cos +0.755.

Finger-extension headroom left by the grasp fitter

Mount-to-pad distance at the closed grip; full extension is 68.11 mm

The deployed hands have no CEM run; their grasp comes from probe_real_v1_carry._grip_from_fit, a geometric IK fit that takes the deepest reachable palm height. The turn draws on finger extension, and the fitted grasps start it with 0.44 to 1.26 mm of headroom against the CEM grasp’s 2.74 mm (the table rounds extensions to 0.1 mm).

At 4 mm the grasp lifts on three pads at 8.79 N, above the reference’s 5.55 N, and still drops the tool by the next seam, so finger travel limits the turn at that grip force. Future work

  • Headroom constraint in the fitter. Reject a pose that leaves under about 2.5 mm of the 68.11 mm chain, and widen the palm-height search beyond the 8 mm clamp, inside which the constraint cannot be met. If the constrained fit on rv05_manual_stored does not reproduce the reference chain, headroom is not the cause.
  • Headroom against outcome across hands. The 2.74 mm comes from one CEM grasp; measure headroom against reorientation outcome on the eight deployed hands before using it as a gate.
  • Set-down seam. Re-run the held cells of D3, D5 and D6 with the descent instrumented, to separate the tool toppling out of the pads from the arm driving the wrist into the table.
  • Pivot over each hand’s bench band. A clip of 0.50 lies below the bench band of four of the eight hands; sweep the pivot over each hand’s own band.
