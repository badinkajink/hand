Terms and metrics

Chain
The simulated UR5e task: grasp the screwdriver lying on the table, carry it, turn it upright against the table, release it, move the palm over it (re-index), re-grasp it and gait it.
Set A, set B
A: the eight hands bench-tested for reorientation. B: the eight promoted by the Sobol-8192 screen. sv1_w6689 is in both.
Squeeze
Commanded interference of the pads with the tool at the grasp, in mm.
Stand
A run that passes the stand gate stood_ok: a tool–table contact, tilt below 14° and the tool within 10 mm of its rest height.
Upright seam
The step at which the maneuver reports the tool vertical.
Held turn
A stand with two or more pads on the tool and over 1 N of pad force at the upright seam (held_reorient).
Tilt, reorient
Tilt: angle of the tool axis from vertical, in degrees. Reorient: 90° minus the tilt at the upright seam, mean ± SD over the stands.
Lost in carry, standing, at handover, gaiting
The first gate a run fails, in order: carry, stand, re-grasp after the re-index, gait. Complete: passes all four.
Close probe
The grasp closed on the lying tool with the palm still. Rise: upward displacement of the tool, in mm. Pad: pad force, in N. Non-pad: contacts on anything but a pad.
Carry, stands, chain (k/n)
Runs, of n at the hand's best squeeze, that pass the carry gate, the stand gate and every gate.
Max pads at upright
Most pads on the tool at the upright seam over the hand's stands.

2 mm loses 24 runs in the carry; 6 mm loses 32 at the handover. Complete chains peak at 2 mm ({{OK2}}) and 4 mm ({{OK4}}); a squeeze chosen per hand gives {{BEST}}. Two handover sweeps did not raise the count. Scanning the gait palm height against the open ring gives 21 chains against 44 for the closed ring (128 runs per arm). Opening the pads to 6–22 mm before the descent changes nothing, since the open ring's IK residual grows one-for-one with the target.

Tool ejection at the deployed 10 mm squeeze

Every deployed plan sets squeeze_mm = 10.0, which drives the pad centres to r_obj + r_pad + gap − squeeze = 12.5 + 10.55 + 1 − 10 = 14.05 mm from the shaft axis, nine millimetres inside the 23.05 mm at which contact begins. The value was fitted on a bench scene where a 100 mm post under the shaft takes the reaction. On a table nothing does, and the pinch drives the tool up and out.

With the palm still, g12_b095 lifts the tool +3.8, +8.9, +12.8 and +18.6 mm at 2, 4, 6 and 10 mm of squeeze while its pad force falls through 10.4, 5.4, 1.8 and 0.5 N. At 10 mm, nine of the sixteen hands lift the tool by more than its 12.5 mm radius, onto thumb_pip_frame.

The fitter's hold probe measures lift against the tool's height before the close, so an 18 mm ejection reads as 18 mm of lift (held ≈ lift_probe + dz), and _grip_from_fit passes hold_min=−1.0, which disables the probe's 20 mm gate.

Future work

  1. Close-probe gate. close_nonpad ≥ 2 selects exactly the hands that eject the tool (sv1_w6689_b050 and _b060, sv1_u5855_b050 at 2 mm), which complete no chain. Apply it in prepare() of scripts/real_v1_chain_hands.py; a removed hand that completes a chain would falsify it.
  2. Squeeze between 2 and 4 mm. The per-hand choice ({{BEST}}/{{N}} against {{OK2}}/{{N}}) rests on 4 runs per cell. Rerun with 8 seeds per cell on the hands that complete any chain; it holds if its margin exceeds the seed spread.
  3. Tip, countersink and relay. Rerun with a nonzero tip_len_mm, place_xy at the countersink and reindex = "relay" (relay hand-over page); the maneuver qualifies only if it then passes held_reorient.
  4. Gear ceiling on the gait gain. The pad ring turns the shaft by 1.684 times the stroke, so a 30° stroke gives at most 50.5° per gait cycle. The value gain_mean_deg, the mean rotation per cycle, reads −67 to −86° on g12 and +188° on u5860, which means a spun or thrown shaft. Count a cycle above the ceiling as a gait loss.
