Corrections, 2026-09-23

The calibrated plant keeps the template’s finger joint damping (0.5 N·m·s/rad), so at kp 0.5 the fingers respond with a 1.04 s time constant; the bench servo is far faster. Every policy on this page trained on it, and with the damping removed all ten drop the tool in their turn. Training pipeline: architecture and the decisions behind it

A policy here is a 9-dimensional residual on a scripted grasp and lift, trained by PPO on 3,072 copies of one hand’s frozen scene in MuJoCo-Warp through mjlab; the tables are read from the first finished job’s configuration.

Stages, from the hardware model to a checkpoint

  1. Hand. One of the eight hands (D1–D8) promoted from the Sobol search over REAL_V1_WORKSPACE and built for the bench, generated from assets/mjcf/real_v1/real_hand.xml with the morphology frozen into the scene (freeze_scene_for_eval).
  2. Grasp. The chain fitter places the three pads at or below the shaft’s equator and writes the open_ik keyframe and the grip anchor, the same pose closed by a 10 mm commanded squeeze.
  3. Plant. apply_measured_plant.py sets the servo fit of 2026-09-02 (finger kp 0.5, kv 0.02, forcerange 0.35 N·m, frictionloss 0.0035, measured link masses; the template’s joint damping 0.5 stays, a 1 s finger time constant), the elliptic cone with impratio 10 and the +25 mm palm plate, in the _cal morphology run with its open-loop reference rollout (best_rollout.npz).
  4. Tool. make_tipmesh_morphology_run.py adds the screw-tip mesh of the chain and the bench (25.6 g) in the _cal_tip run; this tranche is the first to train on it.
  5. Environment. morphohand.rl.env_build makes an mjlab task of the frozen scene, keeping the tool’s contact class and mesh and the scene’s floor, with 2 ms physics and 50 Hz control.
  6. Optimiser. rl_train_cube.py --recipe b_liveA with rsl_rl PPO and the collapse watchdog; a warm start copies the parent’s actor and critic.
  7. Evaluation. 64 nominal and 64 jittered GPU rollouts, CPU replays under the transfer variants, the UR5e chain at both plates, and films.

Lessons from the two tranches

  • Evaluate off the trained pose. A deterministic evaluator on a zero-jitter environment measures the GPU solver; policy_eval_suite.py --spawn-jitter-mm 3 --spawn-yaw-deg 10 --friction-dr gives the number a policy is judged on (*_eval_j3dr.json for every job).
  • Training plant against bench. The tool’s contact class, the floor and the tip had differed between the mjlab environment and the scene since the first mjlab run, and the finger joint damping of both differs from the servo.
  • Arm offset. The 4.9 mm the palm sat below its command at the turn was the menagerie servo easing toward its command (kp/kv = 5 rad/s). A UR5e told its payload holds the palm within about 0.1 mm, and the chain runs the arm at ten times the position gain.
  • Two silent no-ops, both fixed. The friction variants of the zero-shot table were copies of the base scene on every calibrated scene until 2026-09-20 02:00, and --payload-gravcomp also compensated the finger links.
  • Separation term. A term of −10 below 30 mm gives 2–10 mm of index–middle clearance on D1, D3, D5 and D6. It removes the turn on D4 and D7, which turn by running the two fingers together.

Future work

  1. Finger dynamics. Measure a free-air step and a loaded back-drive per joint on the bench (the 2026-09-23 page names both), fit the plant to them, and continue these checkpoints on it; no policy here is a hardware candidate before that.
  2. Turn quality. The continued policies end at cos 0.80–0.96; one 60 M continuation of the D6 separation job on the fitted plant separates convergence from the cost of the jitter.
  3. Inherited settings. Test each setting on D6 at the same budget: tip-lost after 3 against 15 steps; the progress weight at 100 / 300 / 900; a friction band of [0.4, 1.4]; observation noise set from the tracker and servo telemetry; and a residual scale of 1.0 with clip 1.0 (the same ±1 rad with a different exploration std).
  4. D4. Both draws stopped on NaN observations on the g12 tip-mesh scene; inspect the D4 pads against the tip mesh at spawn before another D4 job.
  5. Driver. Apply --seed in rl_train_cube.py (every run is an unseeded draw) and pass --video to the driver’s chain call.
  6. Bench controller. Stream servo telemetry and the AprilTag pose at 50 Hz into the observation and the residual onto the finger set-points, after the tracker’s heading calibration; then a blind actor with a privileged critic.
  7. Clearance. Measure the built index and middle housings with a caliper, then sweep the separation weight (−3 … −10) on D4 and D7.
