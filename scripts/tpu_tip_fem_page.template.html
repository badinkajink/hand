A press in the print model

A screwdriver shaft pushes on the printed fingertip. The outer walls, solid surface layers and gyroid infill all deform. Their stiffness determines how far the shaft moves under a given force. It also determines which parts of the face press hardest on the shaft. That pressure distribution sets the friction torque that resists a twist.

Finite-element analysis divides the tip into small pieces and solves their shared deformation. The calculations below compare several print recipes under the same shaft load. The sphere-pad simulation then uses a contact modulus fitted to each print model. This page records the whole-tip designs studied in October 2026; the printed-fingertip overview follows the pad that was built.
