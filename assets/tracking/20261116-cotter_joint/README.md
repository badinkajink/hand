# Cotter joint between the tag blocks and the tracked objects, 20261116

The tag blocks of `assets/tracking/20261116-tag_blocks/` attached to the cylinder and the sphere without glue (owner, 2026-11-16: "can we think of ways to attach the hex block to the cylinder/sphere without glue? ... some kind of joinery perpendicular to the block shaft ... tight enough to prevent slop but fit enough to insert (and remove in the case of a break)"). Written by `scripts/hardware/tracking_objects_print.py joint`. The blocks, the cylinder body and the sphere's lower half replace those of 2026-11-12 and 2026-11-16; the sphere's upper half and its dowels stay those of 2026-11-12.

Joint. The stem ends in a square taper, its faces at 20 deg to the stem, 6.5 mm across at the object's flat seat. It sits in a socket of the same taper in the object, which is 2 mm deep and continues straight beyond. Above the taper a square post 4.7 mm across has a slot across the stem. The cotter is a pin 2.4 mm wide whose top face rises 1 mm in 5; it is 13.7 mm long and 2.5 mm thick at the stem's axis. It is pushed through the object's slot and the post's slot, under the post's ceiling, which slopes as the cotter does. The cotter rests on the object's slot floor 3 mm above the seat, so pushing it in lifts the post and draws the taper into its socket. The four taper faces then fix the block's position, tilt and turn on the object, and friction keeps the cotter in place. A wedge stays in place while its angle is below the sum of the friction angles on its two faces. The cotter's angle is 11.3 deg, so it stays in place for friction coefficients above 0.10.

Fit. Each cotter takes up the printing error of the joint over its travel: 1.5 mm along the slot each way from its nominal place is 0.3 mm along the stem. The three cotters differ by 0.25 mm in thickness, so together they cover -0.55 to +0.55 mm. Use the cotter whose thick end stops inside the slot's mouth when the block is firm. The stem's -v corner and the socket's are cut flat, so the stem goes in one way only. Turned by 180 deg, it stops 1.2 mm short of its seat. Its slot then lies 0.9 mm below the cotter's tip, and the cotter does not enter it.

Openings. The cotter enters on the cylinder's -x side and on the sphere's +z side. The slot's far end is the opening through which a pin of 2 mm or less pushes the cotter out. The entry is 2.6 x 3.8 mm and the far opening 2.6 x 2.6 mm in section. On the cylinder both lie at the middle of its length, under the grip tape, which can be cut away over them or lifted to take the block off. They span 50 to 71 deg (entry) and 50 to 65 deg (far) from the bottom line; on the sphere 35 to 51 and 35 to 47 deg from the bottom pole.

Assembly. Push the block's taper into the socket, the stem's cut corner toward the socket's cut corner. Slide the middle cotter (two dimples) in from the entry, chamfered tip first, its sloped face toward the object's centre and its dimples away from the cut corner. Press its thick end with a small screwdriver until the block no longer rocks. If the block is firm while the cotter's thick end still stands out of the entry, take the thin cotter (one dimple). If the cotter's tip reaches the far opening's mouth while the block is still loose, take the thick one (three dimples). To take the block off, push the cotter's tip back through the far opening with a pin and pull the block straight out.

Checks. In the CAD assembly the block, the object and the cotter share 0 mm3, on both objects. The shared volume is 0 at the cotter's nominal place and at both ends of its travel, for each of the three cotters. Over its travel the cotter's ends stay 0.56 to 3.56 mm inside the cylinder's surface and 3.15 to 6.15 mm inside the sphere's. It rests at least 1.78 mm on the slot floor on each side of the socket. The post's walls beside its slot are 2.02 mm at the axis and the post's top 1.86 mm above the slot; the thin cotter is 1.51 mm thick at its tip. The black cells of the blocks are those of the second version (shared volume equal to their volume). Every tag face of the black bodies, drawn at 20 px/mm from the mesh, decodes as its own id with corners 0.022 mm RMS from the map.

Mass. The stem with its root, cone, taper and post is 1.02 g (cylinder) and 0.84 g (sphere) of solid PLA, against 0.88 g and 0.71 g in the second version; a cotter is 0.1 g. Weigh each assembled object; the simulation takes the measured mass.
