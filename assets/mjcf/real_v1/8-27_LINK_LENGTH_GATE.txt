# Hardware finger geometry — the 2026-08-27 rebuild, and what is waiting on the real MJCF
#
# STATUS: geometry only. NO policy has been trained or evaluated on any scene in this study.
# Written as a handoff: everything below is what a later agent needs to pick this up.
#
# ORIGIN. Hardware asked whether a 40 mm proximal / 30 mm distal finger is viable, or whether the
# fingers should stay at the "37-40 mm" of earlier iterations. Answering it required correcting
# how this program names finger lengths, then correcting the finger's topology twice.


## 1. TERMS — every length this program has published names a PARAMETER, not a link

The hand has TWO flexion joints per finger (MCP, PIP), so it has two flexing links. The scene
draws the MCP link as two capsules — `<f>_mcp_frame` then `<f>_len_frame` — with NO JOINT between
them, purely so the co-design `len` parameter had somewhere to slide. `set_proximal_length` and
the whole 9-param design space move only the first of those. Read as links:

  build              yaw link   MCP->PIP             PIP->pad     total flexing reach
  m05 (a10 -> b33)      0       75.8 / 77.3 / 80.9   41.0         116.8 / 118.3 / 121.9
  sp25 "25 mm hand"     0       65.0                 41.0         106.0
  a01 lineage           0       73.5 / 74.5 / 74.5   41.0         114.5 / 115.5 / 115.5
  HARDWARE TARGET      32       40                   30            70

So the shipped hand's proximal is 76-81 mm, the "25 mm hand" is a 65 mm one, and nothing has ever
been trained within 25 mm of the 40 mm target. `docs/notes/finger_spec.md` §2 states the parameter
values; read them as parameters, not links.


## 2. TOPOLOGY — two mistakes, both mine, recorded so they are not repeated

**The yaw axis is (1,0,0) — the finger's OWN long axis, a ROLL. It has never changed and must not
be changed.** Yaw turns the flexion plane; that is how the thumb opposes. I changed it to the palm
normal off a badly-worded question of my own and it was wrong.

**The 32 mm yaw link must be PERPENDICULAR to that axis.** Built along +x it lies ON the axis: the
MCP joint sits on the line yaw rotates about, yaw cannot move it, and the two joints are coincident
in everything but their body origins. `yaw_link_dir` defaults to (0,0,-1) — the link drops from the
yaw joint to the MCP joint — and `set_link_lengths` now REFUSES any direction with a component
along the axis.

**The check, and use it on the user's MJCF too:** `yaw_mcp_offset()` in
`scripts/build_link_length_variants.py` returns the perpendicular distance from the MCP joint's
anchor to the yaw AXIS LINE, off the compiled model. It must equal the yaw link length. Zero is
the coincident bug. Every build prints it; the baseline scenes correctly print 0.0/0.0/0.0.
Body origins differing is NOT sufficient evidence — that was the trap both times.

**Drawing.** A capsule extends by its radius past each `fromto` end, so a 40 mm link at r 10 drew
58.5 mm with 10 mm sticking backwards through the yaw link. `--cap-inset` lands the cap ON the
joint; envelope then measures 40.0 / 30.0. Applied at each LINK's outer ends only (the tapered
sub-segments are shorter than their own caps and collapse if both ends are inset). Off by default
because the trained scenes have the legacy convention in the collision geometry the policies
learned against. NOTE FOR CAD: m05 draws a 94 mm envelope on a 75.8 mm proximal — anything sized
off a render or export of the m05 scene is ~18 mm per finger too long.


## 3. THE GATE, AND WHY IT NO LONGER DISCRIMINATES

Constraint: a shorter finger holds the shaft closer to the mounting plane, and the shaft must
stand up in that gap. A 100 mm shaft tilted t off vertical rises 50*cos(t) above the grip, so

    held-cos <= grip depth below the mounting plane / (tool length / 2)

Flush-mounted, no yaw link (fitted palm, m05 mounts, 100 mm shaft) — b33 scores 0.891 for scale:

  76x41 control  86.7 mm depth   ceiling 1.00    longest shaft 173 mm
  40x30          44.1            ceiling 0.88                  88
  30x30          34.9            ceiling 0.70                  70
  25x25          26.3            ceiling 0.53                  53

WITH the 32 mm perpendicular yaw link and fitted mounts, ALL FOUR CLEAR:

  76x41 control  88.9 mm depth   headroom +38.9   ceiling 1.00   longest shaft 178 mm
  40x30 + 32     83.9            +33.9            1.00           168
  30x30 + 32     76.0            +26.0            1.00           152
  25x25 + 32     67.9            +17.9            1.00           136

**The yaw link is what pays for the headroom.** Once it is modelled, the ceiling disappears and
geometry cannot rank these lengths. Only a trained reorient can. Second lever if it ever binds
again: max tool length = 2 x grip depth (`--tool-length`), but shortening the shaft also lightens
it and SIM2REAL_ROBUSTNESS found the hand one-sidedly fragile there (density x0.6 -> hold 0.41).

FREE RESULT: the 32 mm drop clears the ~479 N `mcp_frame`<->`palm` interpenetration that every
baseline scene in this repo carries (finger_spec §3a). Contacts 5 -> 2, both the shaft on the table.


## 4. WHAT HAS AND HAS NOT BEEN MEASURED

MEASURED — CEM grasp, all four lengths, on the OLD scenes (no yaw link, m05 mounts):

  76x41 5.881 | 40x30 5.742 | 30x30 5.736 | 25x25 5.735
  every one: 3.0 tip contacts, 1.00/1.00/1.00 per-finger persistence, ~0 tilt, ~0 drift

Grip is NOT what length decides — 0.15 of spread across a 2.4x range of finger length, consistent
with the program-wide finding that pick-up and hold are solved everywhere. NOT re-run on the
yaw-link scenes; a grasp is a prerequisite for Policy A, so it has to be.

NOT MEASURED — anything with a controller. Zero RL runs reference these scenes. The reorient
question is entirely open on this hand.


## 5. TRAPS HIT IN THIS STUDY

1. **The palm must be FITTED, not just dropped.** Inheriting m05's palm x/y leaves a short-fingered
   hand off-centre over the shaft, and CEM returned a spurious 2-FINGER verdict on 40x30 (thumb
   contact persistence 0.00, score 2.33 against m05's 8.52). Same class as the joint-space keyframe
   bug, one level up. Fixed by solving px/py/pz.
2. **Mount x/y must be fitted too** — they are co-design free variables, and a 70 mm finger wants a
   tighter footprint than a 117 mm one.
3. **The fit objective needs all three terms.** Reach alone is under-determined. Joint-centering
   alone drops the palm until the fingers straighten (ceiling 0.88 -> 0.49). Utilisation alone
   fixes the grasp's shape but not its height (40/30 came back SHALLOWER than 30/30 — a local
   minimum, not a result). Working objective = reach + m05's per-finger utilisation
   (|MCP->tip| / flexing reach, m05 = 0.84/0.75/0.77) + maximise grip depth, MULTI-STARTED from
   reach-scaled mount layouts.
4. **Never `pkill -f` a pattern that appears elsewhere in the same command line** — the relaunch
   half of a compound command counts, and it killed the shell (CLAUDE.md gotcha #9).
5. The Bash tool caps at 120 s regardless of a `timeout N` prefix; launch long builds detached.


## 6. NEXT — WHEN THE HARDWARE MJCF ARRIVES

The mounts and palm height in these scenes are SOLVED, not given: 40x30's fitted mounts are thumb
(-10.8,-7.2) index (+3.3,+30.6) middle (+15.8,-4.6) mm with the palm at 97.1 mm. The real hand's
will differ, and a run against slightly-wrong geometry produces a number that looks real. So:

  1. Take link lengths, mount layout and palm pose FROM the user's model. Do not fit them.
  2. Run the gate on it as a sanity check: `yaw_mcp_offset` must equal the yaw link (not 0), and
     read grip depth / headroom / ceiling.
  3. CEM grasp per design (~5 min each, `scripts/run_linklen_cem.sh`, config pinned to sp25's).
  4. THEN A->B from scratch, n≈3 A draws + n≈3 B draws, ~5.4 GPU-h per design on DeltaAI
     (~92 GPU-h available). **Nothing transfers**: a10/b33 were trained with coincident yaw/MCP
     joints, warmstarting across morphologies is gotcha #5 and NaN'd 15/16 draws in the seed-band
     study, and b33 did not survive even a proximal-length change on the SAME topology
     (hold 0.344, dropped 2 of 3).
  5. Flag parity (this repo has lost two 5-design queues in a day to omitted flags): pin
     terminations to the REFERENCE RUN's config not the recipe's, pass
     `--open-finger-from-keyframe`, and re-derive b33's timings — lift/reorient start 58 and
     tip-loss grace 10 were tuned to m05's palm height and grip depth, both of which move here.
