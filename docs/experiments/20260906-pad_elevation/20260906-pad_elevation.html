Pad contact elevation and tool retention across eight deployed hands

This page claimed that a pad-target angle unblocked two hands and took full chains from 18 of 48 to 30. Those chains stood the screwdriver on its handle. The claim is withdrawn; one measurement on the page survives and is kept below.

Superseded by the control diagnosis of the same date · docs/experiments/20260906-control_diagnosis/
Retraction

probe_real_v1_chain._av() folds the tool's axis to always point up when tip_len is 0, and real_v1_chain_hands._cell passes tip_len=0.0 for every table-stand cell. tilt_deg is then arccos|cos| and a screwdriver standing on its handle reads as a perfect 0.00° stand. Every stand count on the original page came from that mode.

Re-scored on the signed cosine, 952 of 967 of those stands were handle down — 180° from the task. The elevation sweep, the grip-loop sweep and the straddle×depth grid are all void as reported. The air-mode results on the original page were signed and are not affected; they said 0 stands in 96, which was the honest number all along.

The cause of the drops was found the same day and is not the pad elevation: the pivot height axis_k decides which end of the tool goes down, and the grasp fitter leaves the fingers with no extension left to turn with. See the diagnosis page.

Fz,pad is the vertical component of the summed pad contact force. On six of the eight hands it is +0.241 N, the screwdriver's weight (0.240 N) to three figures. On sv1_w6689_b060 it is −3.463 N: its pads press the tool down with fourteen times its weight and it stays put only because the table is underneath. sv1_w0099_b100 is the same failure at one tenth the magnitude.

The consequence drawn from this on the original page — that moving the pads below the equator unblocks the chain — is what is retracted, because it was scored on handle-down stands. The contact geometry itself stands, and fit_real_v1_pose.tip_targets documents the wedge-sign rule it measures.

What the numbers actually are

signed cosine, tip down is +1

Across the air-mode runs of the same day, which are signed because they carry a countersink and therefore a non-zero tip_len: 1 complete chain in 352 rollouts. A subsequent per-hand sweep over the pivot height and the residual clip — 432 rollouts, clip 0.40 to 1.15 against axis_k 0.15/0.25/0.35 — completed 0. Sixty of those 432 held the tool at reoriented on two or more pads at or above its weight, and the best cell reached cos +0.83 at 0.41 N against the reference configuration's +0.994 at 10.47 N.

The reference is rv05_manual_stored, which chained 169 of 169 on 2026-09-03 and is now pinned by tests/test_chain_reference.py.
