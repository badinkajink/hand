Training curves

Lessons from the two tranches

  • Evaluate off the trained pose. A deterministic evaluator on a zero-jitter environment measures the GPU solver, not the policy. policy_eval_suite.py --spawn-jitter-mm 3 --spawn-yaw-deg 10 --friction-dr is now the number a policy is judged on, and the driver writes it for every job (*_eval_j3dr.json).
  • The training plant was not the calibrated plant. Three defects between the frozen scene and the mjlab environment (tool contact class, floor, tip) had been there since the first mjlab run; the CPU replay, the chain and the bench comparison all ran on the scene. Any earlier sim-to-sim gap is confounded by them.
  • Reading the arm. The 4.9 mm the palm sat below its command at the seam was the menagerie servo settling (slow pole kp/kv = 5 rad/s), not load: with the payload declared a UR5e holds ~0.1 mm, and the chain now runs the arm at ten times the position gain.
  • Two silent no-ops. The friction variants of the zero-shot table were copies of the base scene on every calibrated scene until 2026-09-20 02:00 (the variant matched the template’s μ 2.4), and --payload-gravcomp had been compensating the finger links as well as the hand. Both fixed.
  • The separation term. −10 below 30 mm buys 2–10 mm of index–middle clearance on D1/D3/D5/D6 and takes the turn away from D4 and D7, which turn by running the two fingers together; on the matched plant the continued policies sit at 16–29 mm without it.
  • Unattended follow-ups. Two of three scheduled session wake-ups never fired; the page build has to live in the detached driver (or a watcher beside it), not in the session.

  2. Turn quality on the matched plant. One 60 M continuation of the D6 separation job decides which.
  3. The bench controller. A policy-driven station loop (servo telemetry + the AprilTag pose at 50 Hz → the 66-dim observation → the residual on the finger set-points), after the tracker’s x/y heading calibration. Nothing here has run on hardware.
  4. The privileged-critic blind actor (proprioception + motor load + history, critic with the pose), from scratch on the matched plant, once a sighted policy has been shown to transfer.
  5. Clearance threshold from a caliper on the built index and middle housings, then the separation weight sweep (−3 … −10) on D4 and D7 only.
  6. The friction rows of the 2026-09-17 D6 page re-run under the fixed variant (GPU).
