# DIP link with a clamped five-chip magnetometer board: design specification

2026-10-18. Generated by `scripts/dip_link_spec.py` from `spec.json` (every number with its source). Model values unless marked as measured; nothing here is designed or printed yet.

The new DIP link puts the five chips' package tops on the printed tip's back face, removing the 3.0 mm plate between them; the readout model then resolves 0.173 N at the contact point with four magnets and 0.062 N with five (85A, 30 % gyroid), against 0.354 and 0.143 N now. One micrometre of board motion toward the magnets reads as 0.160 N, so the board is clamped by screw preload and located by rigid features, independent of the tip. At this placement J1 overlaps the MCP link by 1.45 mm within the PIP range and only a tab of 4.9 mm or less keeps 0.5 mm clearance, so the tab is cut and the cable soldered, or the board is flipped. The board's four holes lie 0.6 mm inside the tip's side planes, which shapes every clamp.

## Terms and frames

- F: finger frame of the index finger at zero joint angles (scripts/build_cad_v1_finger.py), mm: x along the yaw axis toward the dorsal side, y along the PIP axis, z up; the straight finger hangs along -z.
- PIP angle: q in degrees, positive in flexion (the tip moves toward -x); range -18 to 92° (model).
- Touch placement: the board with its chips' package tops on the tip's back face.
- Gap: distance from the tip's back face to the chips' sensing plane, 0.42 mm under the package top, mm; negative inside the tip.
- Force resolution: Cramér-Rao bound (one standard deviation, N) of the normal force at the contact point from one 141 Hz sample of the five chips, with the contact position and a uniform background field unknown (scripts/tpu_tip_magnet_sensing.py).
- False force: normal force or shear (N) that the readout's estimator (normal and shear force, contact position and background field unknown) reads when the board moves 1 µm relative to the magnets, along x (toward them), y or z.
- Keep-out: the volume that the MCP link and the PIP servo sweep in the DIP link's frame over the PIP range.
- Clearance: least signed distance (mm) from the swept parts' surface to a part fixed to the DIP link; negative values overlap, by that depth.
- Design loads: 10 N normal and 5 N tangential at the contact point, and a 1 N pull on the cable.
- Board frame: (u, v) of the ReSkin 5X files, U1 at the origin; on the finger u = y_F, v = -(z_F + 78.16), U1 over the contact point, U2 toward the tip's end, the tab toward the PIP joint; at the touch placement the FR-4's component face is at x_F 7.35.
- Placements: plate (now), touch, recessed and flipped, defined under the force-resolution table.

## Board distance and force resolution

Force resolution at the contact point (N) by magnet count and gyroid infill, and the static field at U1 (mT), per placement; magnet motions from the readiness study's finite elements of the 85A block.

| Plate | 3.42 | 0.354 | 0.143 | 0.601 | 0.294 | 3.2 / 4.3 | 7 / 7 |
| Touch | 0.42 | 0.173 | 0.062 | 0.287 | 0.123 | 5.5 / 7.7 | 6 / 5 |
| Recessed | -0.48 | 0.134 | 0.048 | 0.223 | 0.094 | 6.5 / 9.3 | 5 / 4 |
| Flipped | 1.08 | 0.205 | 0.075 | 0.341 | 0.149 | 4.9 / 6.7 | 6 / 5 |

- Plate: the readiness scenes: the board on the dorsal face of the DIP link's 3.0 mm plate, chips toward the plate.
- Touch: chip tops against the tip's back face, the FR-4 behind them.
- Recessed: the FR-4's component face on the tip's back face, the chips and capacitors in 1.0 mm pockets of the TPU.
- Flipped: the FR-4's bare face on the tip's back face, the chips and J1 facing away from the tip.

Below GAIN_SEL 7 the datasheet gives no noise, so those resolutions are lower bounds. The readout's estimator (normal and shear force, contact position and background field unknown) reads one micrometre of rigid board motion relative to the magnets as 0.160 N of normal force along x (toward the magnets), 0.041 N of shear along y and 0.130 N of shear along z (four magnets, 30 %, touch); the magnets move 13.2 µm per newton of normal load.

## Joint ranges, loads, fasteners and printer

- PIP range: model -18..92°; driver calibration finger 0 -18..86.72°; finger 1 -2.93..92°; finger 2 -16.11..77.05°.
- Design loads: 10 N normal and 5 N tangential at the contact point, 1 N on the cable. HOM cells on the CAD hand reach 0.18 N m at a joint (summary.json cells, peak_tau_Nm), 6.8 N at the 26.61 mm from the PIP axis to the contact point; the D6 policies grip the screwdriver with 11.6-18.2 N in total (summary.json policies, grip_N).
- Fastener holes in the CAD: 1.60 mm, the four horn-arm screw holes of the DIP link (y -20.15, z -58.05..-66.05); 1.80 mm, servo-tab pilot holes, M2 thread-forming (carriage, yaw link, MCP link); 2.30 mm, the horn's centre screw (DIP link, y -22.45); 2.40 mm, M2 clearance (carriage rail block); 3.30 mm, M3 clearance: the DIP plate's screw into the CAD insert; 5.20 mm, the slotted bore for the 5 mm shaft. The board's holes take M2 (0.20 mm radial clearance) or M1.6 (0.40 mm); R10 sets the materials. Catalogue sizes: M2 cheese head 3.8 x 1.3 mm, M1.6 cheese head 3.0 x 1.0 mm, M2 nut 4.0 mm across flats x 1.6 mm, M2 x 3 brass insert 3.6 mm outside in a 3.2 mm hole.
- Printer: Original Prusa XL 5T (five toolheads, 0.4 mm nozzles, 360 x 360 x 360 mm); PLA or PETG for rigid parts; TPU 85A and PLA or PETG can share one print (five tools); links as built: PLA at 15 % infill (owner, 2026-10-13).
- Mass: DIP link 4.92 g, board 0.64 g.

## Candidate clamps

- Sandwich through the board's holes, screws from the component side: Dorsal backing plate (part of the DIP link) with brass inserts; the board on it located by two printed pins; a 0.9 mm shim frame over the component side with windows; M1.6 or low-head M2 screws through the shim and the board's holes into the inserts. The heads (3.0-3.8 mm) sit at |y| 6.1-9.9 mm and overlap the tip's 14.8 mm width by 0.9-1.3 mm: the tip needs four notches at its back edges, or narrower heads. For: preload on all four corners; board and shim stay when the tip comes off. Against: tip notches; screw heads beside the tip.
- Sandwich with screws from the dorsal side into the frame: Screws through the backing plate and the board into bosses of a component-side frame beside the tip; the bosses (3.2-5.6 mm) overlap the tip's width by 1.0-2.2 mm. For: heads on the dorsal side. Against: bosses beside the tip widen the contact footprint or need tip notches.
- Edge clamp without the holes: The board slides into a pocket of the backing plate; lips over its outer 1.4 mm (|u| 8.6-10.0 mm, clear of U3 and U5, which reach |u| 8.5 mm) and a screwed bar across the proximal margin preload it. For: no screws near the chips. Against: lips add 0.8-1.0 mm per side; preload depends on the lip's stiffness.
- Rigid frame co-printed with the tip: A PLA or PETG frame printed with the 85A tip at its back edges and mount face; the board is sandwiched between this frame and the DIP link's backing plate, located there by pins. For: one set of screws locates tip and board. Against: the board's clamp opens at each tip exchange; TPU-PLA interface strength unmeasured.

## Owner decisions

- Tab: cut the connector tab and solder four wires to it, or keep J1 and flip the board (chips away from the tip: sensing plane 1.08 mm, four-magnet 30 % resolution 0.205 N against 0.173 N at the touch placement).
- Recessed chips: pockets in the TPU's back face for the chips and capacitors put the sensing plane 0.48 mm inside the back face (four-magnet 30 % resolution 0.134 N; static field at U1 6.53 mT, GAIN_SEL 5); the tip's outer surface stays, its back face changes.
- Tip notches: whether the tip's back edges may be notched for screw heads or bosses beside the board's holes.
- Thinner TPU behind the magnets (moving the board into the tip's volume) changes the tip's stiffness and needs new finite elements; this specification keeps the tips' 17.0 mm depth.
- Fasteners: the screws, inserts and nuts in stock (M1.6, M2; brass, nylon or titanium).
