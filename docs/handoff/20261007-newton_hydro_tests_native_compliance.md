- [ ] 11. Native compliance, candidate (e): rolling bristles (owner's idea, 2026-10-07). Give each pad sphere a ball joint at its centre on the fingertip, so the centre and the pressure law stay as they are, and resist its rotation with a spring k_theta = k_t rho^2 (k_t the per-sphere shear stiffness from G and the area per sphere): the contact point then moves elastically by rho*theta before the sphere's Coulomb contact slips. This is the brush model: partial slip from the patch edge inward and a Mindlin-type hysteresis loop, with friction made stiff (impratio 1000 or noslip) so creep does not mask it.
