MorphoHand · updated 2026-10-09

Documentation of the SR² Hand

SR² Hand is a three-finger robot hand whose finger bases ride on XY gantries: six gantry coordinates set the finger layout (the morphology) and nine servos move the fingers. We search layouts and controllers together in simulation, build the chosen hands on the bench, and measure whether the simulated ranking of the hands holds, on one task: turning a 24.5 g screwdriver from flat to vertical and then driving a screw with it.

Topics

Morphology and design search

Of 8,198 sampled finger layouts, 227 passed the simulated screens and eight (D1–D8) were built. They held the tool in 48 of 77 bench trials; over the eight hands, the Spearman correlation of the simulated and measured final tool angle is . In simulation, the layout with the largest workspace for a cylinder or sphere puts the finger mounts 35–37 mm outside its surface.

Hand, plant and bench

Each finger joint is an SCS0009 servo that lags its command by 0.0186° per unit of load and cuts out at a protective torque; under a grasp the yaw joints reach 0.44–0.90 of the command. The servo model reproduces 177 tracked bench runs within 2.89°, and two AprilTags give the tool's pose to 0.017°.

Pages: servo identification · servo model refit · tool tracker.

Reorientation mechanisms and control

Rotating the three contacts as one rigid body about an axis above them stands the screwdriver up open loop, to about 8° from vertical in simulation; the paper's bench results use this plan. On the servo model fitted to the bench it turns the tool 27–57°. Gaits, a gravity swing and hand-object control are the alternatives studied.

Pages: fixed-contact turn · eight deployed hands · hand-object control.

Reinforcement-learning policies

PPO policies trained in MuJoCo-Warp add a learned correction to a scripted lift and turn; none has run on the hand yet. D6 policies trained with sphere-pad fingertips keep the tool in 216 of 220 replays of their finger commands in three other simulators, and those trained with point contact in 3 of 132.

Pages: four contact models · policies across the hands.

Grasp-to-gait chain and sim-to-real

In simulation the whole task (grasp, lift, learned turn, set-down in a countersink, re-grip and a gait that turns the screw) runs as one rollout with the hand on a UR5e arm; 12 of 20 rollouts on D5 and D6 completed, on a servo model since replaced. The hand-over from the turn to the gait loses the most rollouts. The bench has run only the grasp and the turn.

Pages: seam and countersink · chain with the learned turn.

Fingertip contact model

Covering each fingertip with 1 mm MuJoCo contact spheres reproduces the friction torque of a soft fingertip: on a two-pad pinch the pads slip within 0.1 % of the force of Drake's hydroelastic model and spin 6 % under its torque, at 15 µs per step against Drake's 1,298 µs.

Pages: sphere-packed pads · comparison bed.

Reading the pages

The overviews in docs/overviews/ open from a clone in any browser. The 55 dated result pages in docs/experiments/ are Git LFS files, which open as a short pointer text until fetched (about 120 MB):

git lfs install
git -c lfs.fetchexclude= lfs pull --include="docs/experiments/**/*.html"

INDEX.md lists them by date, and each page names the script that rebuilds it.

Code

src/morphohand/ holds the simulation, RL, sampling and bench-replay code, scripts/ the experiment and page scripts (mapped in scripts/README.md), and assets/mjcf/real_v1/real_hand.xml the hand model. README.md describes the environment and the names of hands and policies.
